## Highlights

- Fixed IBKR SPB-8 security start quantity reconstruction for transfer-heavy statements. Transfers now preserve signed `Qty` values, ignore cancelled rows with `Code=Ca`, and skip non-movement `Transferred Lot:` detail rows.
- IBKR Open Positions reconciliation now works by ISIN when available and includes signed Transfers alongside Mark-to-Market prior quantity and Trades/Order deltas. This prevents false manual-review warnings for same-ISIN listing movements such as internal InterDepot transfers.
- Open Positions reconciliation mismatches now use the structured `IBKR_OPEN_POSITION_RECONCILIATION_MISMATCH` diagnostic instead of appearing both as generic manual-review rows and unclassified warnings.

## IBKR transfer handling

- ACATS transfer-in rows continue to reduce reconstructed beginning quantities as external inflows.
- InterDepot movements between symbols that share the same ISIN now net naturally at the ISIN level, including positive and negative signed quantities.
- Cancelled transfer rows are excluded from SPB-8 and reconciliation calculations, while remaining transfer issues continue to surface as review diagnostics when the row cannot be resolved safely.

## Documentation
